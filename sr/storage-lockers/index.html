WIFIGATE use case

Smart Access for Storage Spaces and Lockers

Every renter opens the site gate, the building door and their own locker from their phone, only during the hours you allow. When the rental ends, their access ends with it: no keys to collect and no codes to change.

WhatsApp Key benefits

A personal permission for every renter

No shared code that former renters still remember. Each renter gets access of their own, and removing it takes a moment when the contract ends or a payment is overdue.

Access hours you set

Allow entry only during the site's opening hours, or give a renter a wider window. A mover or a family member can get one-time access for a single day.

Know who came in and when

Every opening is recorded with the person and the time, so a question about a visit is answered from the history instead of from security footage.

Key benefits

  • Open the site gate, building door and locker from the phone
  • A personal permission for every renter
  • Access limited to opening hours or a custom window
  • One-time access for movers and family members
  • Remove access the moment a rental ends
  • History of who opened and when
  • No shared codes, keypads or keys to change
  • No SIM card or internet connection at the door
