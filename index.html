What is WIFIGATE?

Access without the usual friction.

WIFIGATE is a WiFi gate access control system for opening gates, doors, parking barriers, garage doors, and electric roller shutters from a phone. It brings everyday and guest access into one secure platform.

No SIM, No Router

WIFIGATE works directly with the device, with no SIM card, internet plan, or external Wi-Fi router required.

Stability

A streamlined architecture keeps access responsive and dependable while supporting secure, private, encrypted operation.

Quick Guest Invite

Send a secure guest invitation in seconds so visitors can enter without calls, shared remotes, or manual coordination.

90-Day History

Review the last 90 days of access activity for clearer visibility into gate usage and recent entry events.

Auto Open

Let approved users open the gate automatically when they arrive for a smoother, more convenient hands-free experience.

Up to 10 Scheduled Events

Create recurring access schedules and automated actions directly in the system to match how your site operates.

Digital RF Remote Learning

WiFi gate explained

WiFi gate access, clearly explained.

Straight answers about installation, connectivity, costs, and guest access.

What is a WiFi gate access control system?

A WiFi gate access control system lets authorized people open gates, doors, parking barriers, garage doors, and electric roller shutters from a phone. WIFIGATE brings these access points into one secure platform.

Does WIFIGATE need a SIM card or external Wi-Fi router?

No. WIFIGATE communicates directly with the installed device, so the access point does not need a SIM card, internet plan, or external Wi-Fi router.

Does WIFIGATE require a monthly subscription?

No. You pay once for the WIFIGATE device and installation, with no monthly subscription, renewal cycle, or ongoing platform fee. WIFIGATE Automation for commercial use is the only exception and is available by subscription.

Can WIFIGATE provide temporary guest access?

Yes. You can send a secure guest invitation limited to the dates and times you choose, making it suitable for homes, offices, hotels, Airbnb properties, and other managed entrances.
