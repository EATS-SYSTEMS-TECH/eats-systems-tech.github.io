How it works

From booking to open gate, automatically

Three steps, and then it runs on its own for every reservation.

1

A booking comes in

Your booking or property-management system tells WIFIGATE a new reservation is confirmed.

2

Access is created automatically

WIFIGATE issues a secure, time-limited invitation for exactly the right gate, door or garage, valid only for that stay.

3

Your guest walks straight in

They open the entrance from their phone. When checkout arrives, the access expires by itself, nothing to collect or cancel.
