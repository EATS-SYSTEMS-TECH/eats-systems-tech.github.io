Pricing

Simple plans for WIFIGATE Host

Pay per active system, with a monthly minimum of one system. The WIFIGATE controller itself never needs a subscription.

Monthly prices in US dollars (USD), before tax, per active system, band by band: the first 14 systems cost $6 each, systems 15–49 cost $5 each, and every system from the 50th costs $4. For example, 20 systems: 14 × $6 plus 6 × $5, $114 per month. Account minimum: $6 per month, even with no linked system. No platform fee and no charge per guest invitation. Volume quotes are available for 250+ systems.
