facts from the shared template and independent examples of every benefit-card
role. Keep these facts immutable when validating the replacement.

directional dark overlays, content anchored inside the visible viewport, top

Highlights keep the ruled three-column grid down to 961px, one column at 960px,
tablet gutter at 1024px, square icons, compact headings and readable copy.
Sections retain their padding/header geometry. Benefits retain the public
bento palette and white/blue-wash treatment, card spans, shadows, typography,
tags and lead illustration. Model shared structural responsibilities once;
keep compatibility selectors at their existing cascade strength. Do not
regenerate the former declaration list or use formatting as an ownership fix.

mirror their rotation and glow in RTL, wrap narrow row badges and status,
retain roster checkmarks, history chips, invite glyphs and schedule geometry.

allowed; reduced motion removes that lift. At 1024px cards use two tracks with
an odd final card spanning both; at 620px use one track. Hero mobile changes
are inclusive at 768px, full-width action stacking inclusive at 620px.

Keep high-contrast colors/borders, forced-color outlines, print ink and loss of
decorative shadows/rotation. Shared header/footer skins remain elsewhere.
Acceptance samples widths 320/620/621/768/769/960/961/1024/1025/1440, both
directions, normal/150%-text/high-contrast/reduced-motion, pointer and keyboard
states, print/forced colors and Bengali/Japanese/Arabic language guards. Hash
actual computed rendering, including pseudo-elements; keep the old source only
in private evidence and Git history. Retain failure evidence without replacing
the frozen expectations. Run existing presentation contracts and separately
verify complete generated output parity. This is development, not publication.
