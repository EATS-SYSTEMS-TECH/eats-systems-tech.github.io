WIFIGATE use case

Smart Access Control for Sports Facilities

Members, coaches and guests enter on their own permission and within opening hours. No shared key, and no code going around a WhatsApp group.

WhatsApp Key benefits

Access opens with the booking

Whoever booked a court for a given hour gets a permission that opens then and closes at the end of it. Nobody has to wait at the gate to let them in.

A member, a coach and a guest are not the same

Each type of user gets their own permission: a standing member, a coach who comes on set days and a one-off guest. All on one list, with no key changing hands.

Know who was on site and when

When traffic changes through the day, a record of entries helps operations, settles questions about incidents, and confirms the site was locked at the end of the day.

Key benefits

  • Suitable for padel courts, tennis courts, gyms, and training complexes
  • Access by operating hours, bookings, or permissions
  • Management for members, coaches, employees, and guests
  • Less dependence on shared keys and codes
  • Fast, convenient entry from the phone
  • Suitable for complexes with changing operating hours
  • Temporary permissions for events, classes, or training sessions
  • Access history for control and operations
